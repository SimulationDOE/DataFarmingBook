This site provides a textbook and supporting materials illustrating how to apply
and analyze designed experiments for simulation models. 
Prereqs: basic probability & statistics, familiarity with simulation modeling.

The authors reserve copyright, but have chosen to make this material available
at no charge. End-user's access privileges are described under the Free Software
Foundation's "copyleft" license. By making this content free, we seek to relieve
students of any economic burden and empower instructors to pick and choose
chapter content that enhances their own presentation of this material. Check back
for updates, we plan to expand or revise based on reader feedback.

Download DataFarmingBook.pdf and any or all additional materials of interest to you,
either using the "git clone" command if you're familiar with it or by clicking on
the item(s) of interest and clicking the dowload icon when the material is displayed.

Work smarter, not harder, and get more out of your simulation models than you ever
thought possible!
